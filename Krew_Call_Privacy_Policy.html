Krew Call only collects the name you choose to provide. This may be your real name, a nickname, or any other name you wish to use.

We do not collect:

  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers

Push Notifications

If you choose to enable push notifications, Krew Call uses Apple's Push Notification service (APNs) to deliver notifications to your device.

Apple provides a device token so notifications can be delivered. This token is used only for sending notifications through Krew Call and is not used for advertising or to identify you personally.

You can enable or disable notifications at any time through your device's Settings.

Children's Privacy

Krew Call is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
